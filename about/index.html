About

Owner-operated, on purpose.

When you call Recio Solutions, the person who answers is the person responsible for your cargo.

Recio Solutions LLC is a medical courier company based in Central Florida and serving the entire state, founded and operated by Yeffrey Recio. The company was built on a simple observation: healthcare facilities don't leave their courier because of price. They leave because of a missed STAT pickup, an unanswered phone, or a driver who treated a specimen like a package.

So the operation is designed around reliability. One accountable owner. Documented chain of custody on every run. Compliance paperwork ready before a sales conversation, not assembled after one. The certifications below are not marketing. They are the entry ticket to handling your cargo, and we treat them that way.

If your facility is being treated as a rounding error by a national courier, that is exactly the account this company was built to win.
