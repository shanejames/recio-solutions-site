Services

What we move, and how it gets there.

Every service runs under the same discipline: verified pickup, documented custody, timestamped proof of delivery.

Clinical Specimen Routes

Scheduled daily and weekly pickups from clinics, physician offices, urgent care centers, and surgical centers to reference and specialty labs. Blood, urine, tissue, and swab specimens handled under universal precautions, every specimen treated as infectious regardless of the label.

Routes run on a fixed schedule with agreed pickup windows. If your current courier treats your afternoon pickup as optional, this is the service to ask about.

Recurring · Fixed Windows

STAT and On-Demand

Urgent point-to-point runs for time-critical specimens, medications, and documents. STAT runs are dispatched immediately and driven direct to the destination. Your cargo never waits on another stop.

You get a confirmed pickup time when you call, and a delivery confirmation when it lands.

Direct Drive · No Batching

Pharmacy Delivery

Prescription delivery from independent and long-term care pharmacies to homes, assisted living, and skilled nursing facilities. Recipient verification at hand-off and documented proof of delivery on every package.

Same Day · Verified Hand-off

Medical Records and Supplies

Charts, imaging, durable medical equipment, and interoffice transfers between facilities. Reliable filler capacity on existing routes, or scheduled as its own run.

Routed · Flexible

Pricing that matches how the work actually runs

Multi-stop clinic loops are priced per stop, because stops and handling are most of the work. Long point-to-point transfers are priced by distance. Dedicated daily routes run on a flat monthly contract with a written service level agreement.

After-hours, STAT, extended distance, and temperature-controlled handling carry stated surcharges, on the rate sheet, not discovered on the invoice.
