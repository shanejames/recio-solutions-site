Compliance

The page your compliance officer should read first.

A courier is an extension of your regulatory posture. Here is ours, with the citations.

AreaWhat we holdWhat it means for you
HIPAA Documented HIPAA training. Written privacy and security policies covering administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. Breach notification procedure meeting the 60-day rule. We operate as a Business Associate, not a conduit. We sign your BAA during onboarding, before the first pickup. A sample is available on request.
OSHA BBP Bloodborne Pathogens training under 29 CFR 1910.1030, with a written Exposure Control Plan, universal precautions, PPE, and spill procedure. Every specimen is treated as infectious. Your vendor qualification team can review the plan itself, not just a certificate.
DOT UN3373 Training in Category B biological substance transport under 49 CFR 173.199: triple packaging, marking, and handling standards. We know what a correctly packaged specimen looks like, and we refuse one that isn't. That protects your accession, not just our vehicle.
Insurance Commercial auto liability and commercial general liability, bound and current. Certificate of insurance available before the sales call, with your facility named as additional insured on request.
Entity Recio Solutions LLC, a registered Florida limited liability company with an EIN. Your procurement team opens a vendor file for an entity, not a person. We are ready for that file.

Chain of custody

Every run produces a custody record: pickup timestamp, courier identification, seal check, and timestamped proof of delivery with recipient verification. Temperature-sensitive cargo gets logged readings in transit.

Custody records are retained for six years, in line with HIPAA documentation requirements. If you ever need to reconstruct a run, the record exists.
